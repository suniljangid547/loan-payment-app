LoanPay — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: LoanPay is an offline app. Your loans, payments, income, expenses and reminders live only on your phone. We do not collect, transmit, sell or look at your data. There is no account, no analytics and no ads.

1. Who we are

LoanPay ("the app") is a personal loan-repayment planner distributed on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store. Contact: [your-email@example.com] (replace before publishing).

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to schedule your daily plan, EMI-due and side-income reminders locally. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • File access (via system pickers) — only when you export or import a backup file. The app reads/writes only the file you pick.
  • Share sheet — only when you tap "Share plan" or "Export backup".

The app requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or phone-state access.

4. Data that leaves your phone (only by your action)

  • Backup file — a plain JSON file you create and choose where to send (e.g. your own Google Drive, WhatsApp, email). We never receive a copy.
  • Plan share — the text summary you share is composed on your device and handed to the app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp). We never see it.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No account or sign-up, so we hold no identity data.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising, no data selling, no third-party data sharing.
  • No lending. No credit-score checks. No lending-partner referrals.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in system settings) permanently deletes every record on the device. There is nothing to delete on our side because we hold nothing. Your backup files remain wherever you saved them — delete them like any other file.

7. Children

LoanPay is a general-audience finance tool and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds an optional cloud feature (e.g. family sharing), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the feature will stay opt-in and off by default.

9. Legal bases & regional notes

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to process under GDPR, India's DPDP Act 2023, or equivalent laws. If you later use a cloud feature and provide an email address, it will be used solely to enable that feature, at your direction.

2. अनुमतियाँ

ऐप लोकेशन, संपर्क, कैमरा, माइक, SMS या फ़ोन-स्थिति की अनुमति नहीं लेता।
